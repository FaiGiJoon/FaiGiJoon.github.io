About

Helping people through technology

I'm an IT Operations Specialist with a background in network management, system administration, IT support and cybersecurity. In my latest role I was responsible for the network infrastructure, camera systems, servers and workstations of a security company with around 8,000 monitored locations, from configuration and rollout to fixing outages remotely on my own.

Before that I supported employees at global companies like FedEx and Prosus, and taught networks, servers and cybersecurity to MBO Level 4 students at ROC van Amsterdam. I pair technical depth with an analytical, solution-focused approach.

My interest in tech started with Pokémon Platinum and the DeSmuME emulator. Figuring out how an emulator worked was my lightbulb moment, and by 13 I was hooked on computers. In 2015 I did my first IT internship, building and refurbishing PCs and setting them up with the brand-new Windows 10. Seeing someone's "slow" computer feel new again is what made me want to help people through technology.

Next, I want to grow into network engineering, system administration and cybersecurity, making infrastructure more reliable, secure and efficient. Long term, I want to build automation software and new technology that helps hospitals around the world.
