About

Helping people through technology

I'm a System Support Engineer with hands-on experience in IT infrastructure, network configuration and cloud workplace tools. I've supported employees at global companies like FedEx and Prosus, I configure and maintain network equipment at Axis, and I've taught networks, servers and cybersecurity to MBO Level 4 students at ROC van Amsterdam.

My interest in tech started with Pokémon Platinum and the DeSmuME emulator. Figuring out how an emulator worked was my lightbulb moment, and by 13 I was hooked on computers. In 2015 I did my first IT internship, building and refurbishing PCs and setting them up with the brand-new Windows 10. Seeing someone's "slow" computer feel new again is what made me want to help people through technology.

Long term, I want to build automation software and new technology that helps hospitals around the world.
